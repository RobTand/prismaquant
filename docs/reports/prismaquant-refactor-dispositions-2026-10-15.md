selected-output reader, so deleting its hidden map required explicit owned row
materialization. Validation cannot reconstruct the exact DP objective from raw
probe rows because gains, clipping, grouping and auxiliary policy also contribute.

manual entry points; retirement is recorded rather than described as no consumer.

current ambient environment would silently change artifact meaning.
